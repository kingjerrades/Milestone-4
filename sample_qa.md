### 2. Exit load of Groww Liquid Fund
*Factual answer*

> The exit load of Groww Liquid Fund is graded by days since subscription, starting at 0.0070% on day 1, falling daily to 0.0045% on day 6, and becoming nil from day 7. An exit load is a fee charged by the fund when you withdraw your money early.
>
> **Source:** [Monthly Factsheet - September 2026](https://assets-netstorage.growwmf.in/compliance_docs/Downloads/Fact%20Sheet/2026%20-%202027/Monthly%20Factsheet%20Sept%202026.pdf)
