# Disclaimer used in the UI

**Banner (always visible at the top of the app):**

> **Facts-only. No investment advice.** Every answer links its official source.

**Full disclaimer (under "What this assistant covers"):**

> Facts-only. No investment advice. This assistant answers factual questions about four Groww Mutual Fund schemes using only official public documents from Groww Mutual Fund, AMFI and SEBI (plus Groww MF's registrar, KFintech, for statement downloads), and links the source for every answer. It does not recommend, compare returns, or tell you what to buy or sell. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related documents carefully. Please don't enter personal details such as PAN, Aadhaar, phone, email, OTP or account numbers.

**Refusal shown for advice questions (e.g. "Should I buy…?"):**

> I can only share facts, not investment advice, so I can't say whether to buy, sell, or which fund to pick. For personal guidance, consider a SEBI-registered investment adviser; SEBI's investor website explains the basics. I'm happy to share facts like a scheme's expense ratio, exit load or riskometer.

**Shown when personal details are typed:**

> For your safety, please don't share personal details like PAN, Aadhaar, phone number, email, OTP or account numbers here. I haven't stored what you typed. Please ask your question again without those details.

Source of truth in code: `src/disclaimer.py` and `src/router.py` (REPLIES).
