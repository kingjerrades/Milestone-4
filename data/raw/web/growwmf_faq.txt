Q: What is Groww Mutual Fund?
A: Groww Mutual Fund is a new asset management company with a simple mission - creating mutual funds that help you achieve your financial goals. Built by industry experts and the innovative minds behind the trusted Groww platform, we're all about providing you with a smooth and transparent investing experience.

Q: How is Groww Mutual Fund different from Groww?
A: Groww Mutual Fund is responsible for creating and managing mutual funds that work for you. Groww on the other hand, is an investment platform where you can invest in mutual funds of all other fund houses as well, including Groww MF.

Q: What kinds of funds does Groww MF have?
A: We have funds across 4 categories: Equity, Debt, Hybrid, ETFs. Funds are also available in regular and direct formats.

Q: Where can I invest in Groww MF?
A: You can invest in Groww Mutual Fund through: all financial advisors or mutual fund distributors who are empanelled with Groww AMC; designated Investor Service Centers (ISC) of Registrar & Transfer Agents; leading online platforms and stock brokers providing MF investment facilities such as Karvy Fintech, MF Central etc. You can also invest in Groww Mutual Funds through our website (https://gmf.kfintech.com/).
